# Archiverse

A beautiful, Material 3-design reader for Archive of Our Own (Ao3).

## 🌠 Features

Explore a galaxy of stories with these features!

### 📚 Read
- **Read Works**: Dive into your favorite works with a sleek, Material 3 Expressive design.
- **Bookmark Works**: Save your favorite works for easy access later, with full sync with your AO3 account (**Coming soon!**).
- **Organize with Folders**: Create folders to categorize your saved works, making it easy to find what you love.
- **Search Works and More**: Quickly find works, users, and tags with a powerful search feature. More search capabilities **Coming soon!**
- **Download Works**: Download works for offline reading, so you can enjoy your favorites anywhere, anytime. **Coming soon!**

### 🌌 Explore
- **Sign in to your AO3 Account**: First for any AO3 app! Sign in to your AO3 account to sync your bookmarks and follow your favorite authors.
- **Discover New Works**: Find your next favorite work straight from the Discover tab, curating your personalized exploration based on works you read and authors you follow.
- **Trending Works**: Stay updated with the latest trending works, showcasing the most popular stories on Ao3.
- **Explore Collections**: Browse through collections to find curated works that match your interests. **Coming soon!**
- **Explore Users**: Discover new authors and follow their works, keeping up with your favorite creators.
- **Explore Tags**: Dive into the world of tags to find new works that match your interests.
- **Follow Users**: Keep up with your favorite authors by following them and getting updates on their new works. **Coming soon!**

### ✏️ Write

These features are coming soon!

- **Create Works**: Write and publish your own works directly from the app.
- **Edit Works**: Update your existing works with new content or corrections.
- **Manage Collections**: Create and manage your own collections of works.
- **Manage Series**: Organize your works into series for better storytelling.

### 🎨 Customize

- **Custom Themes**: Choose from a variety of themes to personalize your reading experience.
- **Font Options**: Select your preferred font style and size for optimal readability.
- **Layout Preferences**: Adjust the layout to suit your reading habits.
- **Background Color**: Choose a background color that is easy on the eyes for long reading sessions.

## ⁉️ Where is the API code?

Archiverse is provided as a source-available project, meaning certain portions of the app are not open source, including the API code. **The Archive currently suffers from abuse by bots, scrapers, and notably AI firms using AO3's data to train their models without the Archive's nor the authors' consent.** As a result, it has been agreed by me and the Organization for Transformative Works (OTW; the volunteer organization behind AO3) that the API code will not be open source to prevent potential further abuse of the Archive's data.

The UI and local database portions of the app are made source-available to allow contributors to help improve the app's user interface and functionality. If you are interested in contributing, please check out the [Contributing Guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## ❔ Is this app sanctioned by the OTW?

**Yes!** While the OTW does not officially endorse any third-party apps for Archive of Our Own, they have given permission for Archiverse to be developed and distributed at its current form, including certain monetization strategies that will be introduced in the future.

Archiverse is developed in accordance with the [OTW's Terms of Service](https://archiveofourown.org/tos) and [Privacy Policy](https://archiveofourown.org/privacy). In addition, Archiverse is developed to comply with AO3's rate limits and usage guidelines, ensuring that it does not abuse the Archive's resources.
